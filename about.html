Our Story

Our Story

Orion Sky Holiday was born from a simple passion: travel should be exciting, personal, and stress-free. After seeing travelers struggle with rigid group packages, hidden charges, and generic itineraries, we set out to build a bespoke travel agency that prioritizes you.

Based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, we craft tailor-made international journeys and cruise holidays across Southeast Asia, East Asia, the Middle East, and beyond. Every itinerary is fully customizable — built around your schedule, preferred hotels, travel pace, and budget.

Whether it's a romantic Bali honeymoon, a family holiday across Singapore & Malaysia, a cultural exploration of Japan, or a luxury cruise voyage, our team is with you every step of the way with 24/7 on-trip support.
