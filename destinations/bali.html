Why Bali?

Bali is more than a beach destination — it's a living tapestry of Hindu temples, emerald rice terraces, and a creative spirit that pulses through every village.

From the surf breaks of Canggu to the sacred monkey forest of Ubud, Bali blends spiritual energy with laid-back island vibes.

Visa Information

Bali (Indonesia) offers a visa on arrival for many nationalities. Requirements vary by passport — we'll confirm the exact process for your nationality.

  • Visa on Arrival: 30 days for most nationalities, extendable once
  • E-Visa: Available online before travel for eligible nationalities
  • Indian Passport: Visa on arrival available, 30-day stay
  • Processing: On arrival at airport or pre-approved e-visa
  • Documents: Passport validity 6+ months, return ticket, proof of accommodation

Requirements change frequently — we'll confirm everything before your trip.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most

The dry season from April to October is best, with sunny days and lower humidity. July–August is peak season. The wet season (November–March) has afternoon showers but fewer crowds and lower prices.

For a highlights trip, 7–10 days is ideal. To explore thoroughly including Nusa Penida and east Bali, 12–14 days gives a much richer experience.

Bali is generally very safe. Petty theft can occur in tourist areas. Be cautious with ocean swimming — currents can be strong. We'll share practical tips for a smooth trip.

Must-try dishes include nasi goreng, babi guling (suckling pig), bebek betutu (slow-cooked duck) and lawar. Each region has specialties — we'll include food highlights in your itinerary.

Most nationalities get visa on arrival for 30 days. Indian passport holders are eligible. We'll confirm requirements before you travel.

Bali is very family-friendly with welcoming locals, water parks and plenty of activities for all ages. We can design an itinerary that balances adult interests with kid-friendly experiences.

Private drivers are affordable and the most comfortable option. Scooter rental is popular but requires experience. We'll arrange all transport as part of your itinerary.
