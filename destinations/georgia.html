Why Georgia?

Georgia sits at the crossroads of Europe and Asia, offering a culture shaped by thousands of years of winemaking, hospitality and mountain living. From the cobblestoned charm of Tbilisi to the snow-capped peaks of Kazbegi, every corner reveals something new.

The food is extraordinary — khachapuri, khinkali and natural wines — and the warmth of Georgian hospitality is legendary. Best of all, it's incredibly affordable and visa-free for many nationalities.

Visa Information

Georgia offers visa-free entry for over 100 nationalities. Indian passport holders can apply for an e-visa or visit visa-free if they hold a valid Schengen, US, UK, Canada or Australia visa.

  • Visa-Free: Many nationalities (1 year for some, 90 days for others)
  • E-Visa: Available for remaining nationalities
  • Indian Passports: Visa-free with valid Schengen/US/UK visa
  • Documents: Passport validity 3+ months beyond stay
  • Processing: Instant for visa-free, 10 days for e-visa

Requirements change frequently — we'll confirm everything before your trip.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most

May to October is the best period. Summer (Jun-Aug) is ideal for mountain trekking. Autumn (Sep-Oct) brings wine harvest festivals and stunning foliage. Winter is great for skiing and hot springs.

For a highlights trip, 7-10 days covers Tbilisi, Kazbegi and the wine region. To explore thoroughly including Svaneti and the Black Sea, 12-14 days gives a richer experience.

Georgia is very safe for tourists with low crime rates. People are incredibly hospitable. Standard travel precautions apply.

Must-try dishes include khachapuri (cheese bread), khinkali (soup dumplings), lobio (bean stew), churchkhela (grape candy) and the incredible natural wines.

Many nationalities enjoy visa-free entry for up to 1 year. Indian passport holders can enter visa-free with a valid Schengen, US, UK or Canada visa. Otherwise an e-visa is available.

Absolutely. Georgia is very family-friendly with welcoming locals, comfortable accommodation and activities for all ages — from cable car rides to cave tours.

Shared marshrutkas (minibuses) connect most towns. Private drivers are very affordable. The new motorways make road travel comfortable. We'll arrange all transport as part of your itinerary.
