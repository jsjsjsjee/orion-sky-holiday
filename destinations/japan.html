Why Japan?

Japan is a mesmerising blend of ancient tradition and cutting-edge modernity. From the serene temples of Kyoto to the neon-lit streets of Tokyo, every city has its own rhythm.

With world-class cuisine, efficient transport and a culture of deep respect, Japan rewards curious travellers at every turn.

Visa Information

Japan requires a visa for most nationalities. The process is straightforward with proper documentation.

  • Tourist Visa: Required for most nationalities, valid for 14–90 days
  • Visa Waiver: Some countries exempt (US, EU, etc.) — check your nationality
  • Indian Passport: Tourist visa required, 15–30 day stay typical
  • Processing: 3–7 business days through embassy or authorised agents
  • Documents: Passport validity 6+ months, itinerary, bank statements, return ticket

Requirements change frequently — we'll confirm everything before your trip.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most

Spring (March–May) for cherry blossoms and autumn (September–November) for fall foliage are the most popular. Summer is hot and humid but has festivals. Winter is cold but great for skiing and onsen.

For a highlights trip, 7–10 days covering Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka is ideal. To explore thoroughly including rural areas, 12–14 days gives a much richer experience.

Japan can be moderate to expensive. Budget travellers can use hostels, convenience store meals and public transport. Mid-range is comfortable with good hotels and restaurant meals. We'll optimise for your budget.

Must-try dishes include sushi, ramen, tempura, tonkatsu, okonomiyaki and matcha desserts. Each region has specialties — we'll include food highlights in your itinerary.

Most nationalities need a tourist visa. Indian passport holders require a visa — we'll guide you through the process and confirm requirements before you travel.

Japan is excellent for families — safe, clean, efficient and full of kid-friendly attractions. Theme parks, aquariums and cultural experiences keep children engaged.

The Japan Rail Pass offers unlimited travel on most trains. IC cards work on local transport. We'll recommend the best transport options for your itinerary.
