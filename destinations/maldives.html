Why Maldives?

The Maldives is the world's lowest-lying nation — a scattering of 1,200 coral islands where overwater villas float above turquoise lagoons and private beaches stretch to the horizon.

Beyond the luxury resorts, local islands offer authentic Maldivian culture, vibrant coral reefs and some of the best diving on the planet.

Visa Information

The Maldives offers a free visa on arrival for all nationalities. Requirements are minimal.

  • Visa on Arrival: Free 30-day visa for all nationalities
  • Extension: Can be extended up to 90 days at the immigration office
  • Indian Passport: Free visa on arrival, 30-day stay
  • Processing: Granted on arrival at Velana International Airport
  • Documents: Valid passport (6+ months), return ticket, proof of accommodation or booking

Requirements change frequently — we'll confirm everything before your trip.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most

The dry season from November to April is best, with calm seas, clear visibility and sunny skies. The wet season (May–October) has more rain but is great for manta rays and whale sharks, especially in Baa Atoll.

For a luxury escape, 5–7 days is perfect. To explore multiple atolls and local islands, 10–12 days gives a much richer experience. We'll help you choose based on your interests.

Maldives can range from moderate to ultra-luxury. Local island guesthouses offer affordable options, while private resort islands are premium. We'll optimise for your budget.

Snorkeling and diving are the main draws — the marine life is world-class. Overwater villa relaxation, sunset cruises, local island visits and fishing are also highlights.

No visa required — all nationalities get a free 30-day visa on arrival. We'll confirm the latest requirements before you travel.

Many resorts offer family villas, kids' clubs and shallow lagoons perfect for children. We can recommend the best family-friendly resorts and design an itinerary that works for all ages.

Speedboats, seaplanes and domestic flights connect the atolls. We'll arrange all transfers as part of your itinerary.
