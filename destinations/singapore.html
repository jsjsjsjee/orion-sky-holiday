Why Singapore?

Singapore packs an incredible amount into a compact island. Gleaming skyscrapers tower over colonial shophouses, while world-class hawker centres sit beside Michelin-starred restaurants.

It's a city where you can explore lush gardens, shop on Orchard Road, wander through Chinatown and Little India, and end the day watching the skyline light up over Marina Bay — all in a single day.

Popular Places

Iconic neighbourhoods and attractions across Singapore

Marina Bay

The heart of modern Singapore — Marina Bay Sands, the ArtScience Museum and the stunning light and water show at Spectra.

1–2 days

Sentosa

Singapore's island playground — beaches, Universal Studios, S.E.A. Aquarium and luxury resorts in one fun-filled escape.

1–2 days

Gardens by the Bay

Futuristic Supertrees, the Cloud Forest and Flower Dome — a nature experience unlike anything else on the planet.

Half day

Chinatown

Heritage temples, bustling markets and some of the best hawker food — from Maxwell Food Centre to traditional tea houses.

Half day

Little India

Colourful shophouses, spice shops, sari stores and the famous Tekka Centre — a feast for all senses.

Half day

Orchard Road

Singapore's premier shopping belt — luxury malls, designer boutiques and endless retail therapy from one end to the other.

1 day

Visa Information

Singapore is visa-free for many nationalities. Indian passport holders may need a visa — we'll confirm the exact process for your nationality.

  • Visa-Free: Many nationalities (30–90 days)
  • Indian Passports: Visa required, apply online
  • Processing: 3–5 business days
  • Documents: Passport validity 6+ months, return ticket
  • SG Arrival Card: Electronic submission required

Requirements change frequently — we'll confirm everything before your trip.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most

February to April offers the best weather with less rain. However, Singapore is a year-round destination — even during the wetter months, rain typically comes in short afternoon bursts. December is festive but crowded.

For a highlights trip, 4–5 days covers the main attractions comfortably. To explore neighbourhoods deeply and take day trips, 7 days gives a richer experience. We'll help you balance your time based on your interests.

Singapore can be pricey for accommodation and attractions, but food and transport are very affordable. Hawker meals cost xxxxx–500, and the MRT is efficient and cheap. With smart planning, Singapore offers excellent value.

Must-try dishes include Hainanese chicken rice, laksa, char kway teow, chili crab, bak kut teh and kaya toast. Visit hawker centres like Maxwell, Lau Pa Sat and Tiong Bahru for the best local food.

Many nationalities enjoy visa-free entry for 30–90 days. Indian passport holders need a visa, which can be applied for online. We'll confirm the exact requirements for your nationality before you travel.

Absolutely. Singapore is one of the best family destinations in Asia — safe, clean, and packed with kid-friendly attractions like Universal Studios, S.E.A. Aquarium and the Singapore Zoo.

The MRT (metro) is excellent and covers most attractions. Buses, taxis and ride-hailing apps like Grab are widely available. Singapore is very walkable, especially in the core areas. We'll include transport tips in your itinerary.
