Why South Korea?

South Korea is a country of fascinating contrasts. Ancient Buddhist temples sit beside futuristic skyscrapers, and traditional hanbok-clad visitors walk the same streets as K-pop stars.

From the buzzing energy of Seoul's nightlife to the volcanic landscapes of Jeju Island, Korea rewards curious travellers with incredible food, deep history and warm hospitality.

Popular Places

Ancient capitals, coastal cities and volcanic islands

Seoul

A city of palaces, K-pop, street food and buzzing nightlife — where centuries-old traditions meet cutting-edge culture.

3–4 days

Busan

Korea's coastal gem — beaches, the famous Jagalchi fish market, Gamcheon village and stunning temple stays.

2–3 days

Jeju Island

Volcanic landscapes, waterfalls, Hallasan mountain and Korea's favourite honeymoon destination.

2–3 days

Gyeongju

The ancient Silla capital — burial mounds, temples and the remarkable Bulguksa temple complex.

1–2 days

Incheon

Modern airport city with Chinatown, Songdo's futuristic skyline and beautiful coastal parks.

1 day

DMZ

The world's most heavily armed border — a fascinating, sobering glimpse into Korea's divided history.

Half day

Visa Information

South Korea requires K-ETA (Korea Electronic Travel Authorization) for most nationalities. We'll confirm the exact process for your passport.

  • K-ETA: Required for most nationalities, apply online
  • Visa-Free: Some ASEAN and Western countries (90 days)
  • Processing: K-ETA approved within 72 hours
  • Documents: Passport validity 6+ months, return ticket
  • K-ETA fee: Approximately ₹2,500 per person

Requirements change frequently — we'll confirm everything before your trip.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most

March to May (spring) and September to November (autumn) are the best seasons. Spring brings cherry blossoms; autumn offers stunning foliage. Summer is hot and monsoon-heavy; winter is cold but great for skiing.

For a highlights trip, 7–10 days covers Seoul, Busan and one other destination. To explore thoroughly including Jeju and Gyeongju, 12–14 days gives a richer experience. We'll help you balance your time.

South Korea is one of the safest countries in Asia for tourists. Crime rates are very low, public transport is excellent and people are helpful. The only area requiring caution is the DMZ border zone.

Must-try dishes include bibimbap, Korean BBQ (samgyeopsal), tteokbokki, kimchi jjigae, Korean fried chicken and japchae. Street food markets like Myeongdong and Gwangjang are foodie paradises.

Most nationalities need K-ETA (Korea Electronic Travel Authorization) which can be applied for online. Some countries enjoy visa-free entry. We'll confirm the exact requirements for your nationality.

Absolutely. Korea is very family-friendly with excellent infrastructure, clean cities and plenty of attractions for all ages — from theme parks to museums and nature walks.

The KTX high-speed rail connects major cities efficiently. The Seoul metro is world-class. Buses, taxis and ride-hailing apps are widely available. We'll arrange all transport as part of your itinerary.
