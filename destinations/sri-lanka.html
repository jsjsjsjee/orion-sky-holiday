Why Sri Lanka?

Sri Lanka packs extraordinary diversity into a compact island. From the ancient rock fortress of Sigiriya to the rolling tea country of the hill country, every region has its own distinct character.

With world-class wildlife, stunning beaches, centuries-old temples and some of the friendliest people you'll meet anywhere, Sri Lanka is one of Asia's most rewarding destinations.

Popular Places

Ancient cities, hill country and golden beaches

Colombo

The bustling capital — colonial architecture, vibrant markets, rooftop bars and the emerging food scene.

1–2 days

Kandy

The cultural heart — Temple of the Tooth, scenic lake and gateway to the tea country hill station.

2 days

Sigiriya

The iconic Lion Rock fortress — ancient frescoes, mirror walls and breathtaking views from the summit.

1 day

Galle

A beautifully preserved Dutch colonial fort — boutique shops, cafes and ramparts overlooking the Indian Ocean.

1–2 days

Hill country paradise — tea plantations, Nine Arches Bridge, Little Adam's Peak and the famous train ride.

Tea Plantations

Temples

Ancient Buddhist temples, cave paintings at Dambulla and the sacred Temple of the Tooth.

Beaches

Unawatuna, Mirissa, Tangalle — golden sand beaches, whale watching and coconut palm sunsets.

Visa Information

Sri Lanka requires an ETA (Electronic Travel Authorization) for most nationalities. Indian passport holders can get a visa on arrival or apply for an ETA online.

  • ETA: Required for most nationalities, apply online
  • Visa on Arrival: Available for Indian passports (30 days)
  • ETA fee: Approximately ₹2,000 per person
  • Processing: Instant to 48 hours for ETA
  • Documents: Passport validity 6+ months, return ticket

Requirements change frequently — we'll confirm everything before your trip.

The west and south coasts are best from December to March. The east coast is best from April to September. Hill country is pleasant year-round but coolest from November to January. Many travellers choose December to March for balanced weather.

For a highlights trip, 7–10 days covers the main attractions comfortably. To explore thoroughly including the east coast and wildlife, 12–14 days gives a much richer experience. We'll help you balance your time.

Sri Lanka is generally very safe for tourists. The country has recovered well and tourism is thriving. Standard travel precautions apply — be mindful of your belongings in crowded areas.
