Why Thailand?

Thailand is where ancient temples meet turquoise waters. From the grandeur of Bangkok's Grand Palace to the limestone cliffs of Krabi, every region offers a distinct character.

With legendary street food, warm hospitality and incredible value for money, Thailand rewards every type of traveller — from temple-hoppers to beach-lovers.

Visa Information

Thailand offers visa exemption and visa-on-arrival options for many nationalities. Requirements vary by passport — we'll confirm the exact process for your nationality.

  • Visa Exemption: 30–60 days for most nationalities (Indian passport: 60 days under bilateral agreement)
  • Visa on Arrival: Available at major airports for select nationalities
  • Tourist Visa: 60-day single/multiple entry available at embassies
  • Processing: On arrival or pre-approved e-visa
  • Documents: Passport validity 6+ months, return ticket, proof of accommodation

Requirements change frequently — we'll confirm everything before your trip.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most

The best time is the cool dry season from November to February, when temperatures are pleasant across the country. March to May is hot but still good for beaches. The rainy season (June–October) offers fewer crowds and lower prices.

For a highlights trip, 7–10 days is ideal. To explore thoroughly including islands and northern culture, 12–14 days gives a much richer experience. We'll help you balance your time based on your interests.

Thailand is generally very safe for tourists. Petty theft can occur in crowded areas, so standard precautions apply. The biggest safety concern is traffic — crossing roads in cities takes practice. We'll share practical tips for a smooth trip.

Must-try dishes include pad thai, green curry, som tum (papaya salad), mango sticky rice, and massaman curry. Street food stalls are often the best — we'll include food highlights in your itinerary.

Most nationalities get visa-free entry for 30–60 days. Indian passport holders currently get 60 days under bilateral agreement. Requirements depend on your passport — we'll confirm before you travel.

Absolutely. Thailand is very family-friendly with welcoming locals, affordable childcare options and plenty of activities for all ages. We can design an itinerary that balances adult interests with kid-friendly experiences.

Domestic flights are affordable and connect major cities. Trains and buses are comfortable for road travel. Tuk-tuks and songthaews are great for local transport. We'll arrange all transport as part of your itinerary.
