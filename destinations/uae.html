Why the UAE?

The UAE is where ambition meets luxury. Dubai's record-breaking skyscrapers, Abu Dhabi's cultural landmarks and the vast Arabian desert create an experience unlike anywhere else on earth.

With world-class dining, year-round sunshine, duty-free shopping and adventure in the dunes, the UAE caters to every kind of traveller — from luxury seekers to family explorers.

Visa Information

The UAE offers visa on arrival for many nationalities. Indian passport holders can get a 30-day visa on arrival or apply for an e-visa in advance.

  • Visa on Arrival: 30 days for many nationalities
  • E-Visa: Available for Indian passports (14-30 days)
  • Processing: Instant for on-arrival, 3-5 days for e-visa

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most

November to March is the best period with pleasant temperatures (20-28°C). December and January are peak season. Summer months (June-August) are extremely hot but offer great hotel deals.

For a highlights trip, 5-7 days covers Dubai and Abu Dhabi well. To explore all Emirates including Ras Al Khaimah and Fujairah, 10-12 days gives a complete experience.

The UAE is one of the safest countries in the world for tourists. Crime rates are extremely low, infrastructure is world-class and locals are welcoming. Dress modestly in cultural areas.

Must-try dishes include machboos (spiced rice), luqaimat (sweet dumplings), Arabic mezze, shawarma and camel meat. The UAE also has incredible international dining from every cuisine.

Many nationalities get visa on arrival for 30 days. Indian passport holders can also get a 30-day visa on arrival or apply for an e-visa. We'll confirm the exact process for your nationality.

Absolutely. The UAE is extremely family-friendly with world-class theme parks, waterparks, beaches and kids' activities. Dubai and Abu Dhabi have dedicated family entertainment districts.

Dubai has an excellent metro system. Taxis and ride-hailing apps (Careem, Uber) are widely available. For inter-Emirate travel, comfortable buses or private transfers work well. We'll arrange all transport.
